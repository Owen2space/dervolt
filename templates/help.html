USD to KES Conversion

Der-Volt uses real-time USD to KES exchange rates from Open Exchange Rates API, updated every 12 hours.

How It Works

  • Deposits: You deposit in USD, and the system converts it to KES using a favorable deposit rate.
  • Withdrawals: The system converts your USD to KES for withdrawal using the withdrawal rate.
  • Spreads: Different rates apply for deposits vs withdrawals to account for market fluctuations.

Deposit Example (1,000 USD)

USD Amount: $1,000.00
Fee (1%): -$10.00
Net USD: $990.00
Deposit Rate: ~132.90 KES per USD
KES Amount: ~131,571 KES

Withdrawal Example (1,000 USD)

USD Amount: $1,000.00
Fee (1.5%): -$15.00
Net USD: $985.00
Withdrawal Rate: ~126.20 KES per USD
KES Amount: ~124,307 KES

Fee Structure

  • Deposits: 1% fee of the USD amount
  • Withdrawals: 1.5% fee of the USD amount

Rates are updated every 12 hours to ensure you get competitive exchange rates.

How do I install Der-volt on my device?

Der-volt is available as a Progressive Web App (PWA), which means you can install it on your device and use it just like any other app.

Benefits of installing Der-volt as an app:

  • Access Der-volt directly from your home screen
  • Use Der-volt even when you're offline
  • Faster loading times
  • Full-screen experience without browser controls
  • Receive important notifications

On Chrome, Edge, or Android:

  1. Look for the install icon in the address bar or menu
  2. Click "Install" or "Add to Home Screen"
  3. Follow the prompts to complete installation

On iOS (Safari):

  1. Tap the Share button
  2. Scroll and select "Add to Home Screen"
  3. Tap "Add" in the top-right corner
You can also use the install button which appears at the bottom of the page when installation is available.
